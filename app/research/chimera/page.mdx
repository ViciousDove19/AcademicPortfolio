# CHIMERA

*Agentic multimodal decision support for prostate cancer.*

## In plain language

A clinical decision is rarely made from one type of evidence. CHIMERA is an
agentic pipeline that brings histopathology, clinical, and molecular
evidence together to assess prostate cancer risk — and, because the
reasoning is produced by an agent rather than a single opaque model, it can
be evaluated on whether that reasoning trace holds up, not just on whether
the final answer was right.

## Overview

An agentic pipeline that integrates histopathology, clinical, and molecular
evidence to produce prostate cancer risk assessments, evaluated on both
prediction accuracy and the fidelity of the reasoning trace it produces.
