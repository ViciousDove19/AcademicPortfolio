# OpenBUS

<Stamp>In preparation, Medical Image Analysis</Stamp>

*A foundation model for breast and multi-anatomy ultrasound.*

## In plain language

Most ultrasound models are trained from scratch for one anatomy and one
task. OpenBUS asks whether a single encoder, pretrained across many
ultrasound sources and anatomies, can transfer instead of being rebuilt each
time — and whether the masking scheme used during pretraining should be
informed by how ultrasound images are actually formed, rather than applying
generic, uniform random masking.

## Overview

A JEPA-style self-supervised model trained across a curated multi-source
ultrasound corpus, using masking informed by ultrasound image formation
rather than uniform random patches. The aim is a general-purpose ultrasound
encoder that transfers across anatomies and acquisition settings.

## Method

Pretraining follows a JEPA-style (joint-embedding predictive) objective
rather than pixel-level reconstruction, with the masking scheme designed
around ultrasound image formation instead of uniform random patches.
