# PS-MAE

<Stamp>Under review, IEEE JBHI</Stamp>

*Peritumoral masked autoencoding for axillary lymph node metastasis prediction.*

## In plain language

Whether breast cancer has spread to the axillary lymph nodes isn't only written
in the tumour — it shows up in the tissue that surrounds it. Most models are
trained to look at the lesion and crop the rest away. PS-MAE pretrains an
encoder by masking that surrounding margin and asking it to reconstruct it,
so the representation it learns can't ignore the margin the way a
lesion-cropped model does.

## Overview

Nodal status in breast cancer is decided by tissue that sits outside the
tumour, but most imaging models are trained to attend to the lesion itself.
PS-MAE uses a Sobel-guided masking scheme over the peritumoral ring to force
the encoder to represent the margin and surrounding tissue during
pretraining, rather than treating it as background.

## Method

PS-MAE is a masked autoencoder that replaces random patch masking with
peritumoral ring masking and Sobel edge reconstruction targets. This encodes
the clinical prior that the nodal metastasis signal lives in the tissue
surrounding the lesion rather than within it, so the reconstruction
objective directly targets that margin instead of arbitrary patches.

## Data

Evaluated under linear probing on an internal cohort, and on a
class-imbalanced, out-of-distribution external cohort to test whether the
learned representation holds up outside the training distribution.

## Results

Exceeds prior state-of-the-art under linear probing, and holds that margin
on the class-imbalanced out-of-distribution external cohort. Full
quantitative results are pending publication.
